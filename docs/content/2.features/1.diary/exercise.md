# Exercise Tracking

SparkyFitness allows you to track exercise routines with duration, intensity, sets, weights, and calories burned. Reusable workout presets can be assigned to weekdays in **Training > Workout Plans**. Activating a dated plan creates the corresponding future diary sessions through the native workout-plan system.

When a planned preset is later imported as a completed Speediance workout with the same preset and date, the completed provider record replaces the planned placeholder. Reports therefore use the performed Speediance details without showing a second planned copy.

The **Speediance Workout Manager** under Training edits the connected account's complete custom workouts and mirrors every saved definition into a native Sparky workout preset. Warm-up blocks, preset type, repetitions or target RM/fixed weight, rest, and resistance mode remain part of the remote definition. Dated multi-week plans continue to use the normal Sparky workout-plan and diary architecture.

The **Training calendar** beneath Workout Plans shows every canonical completed workout, missed session, and upcoming materialized plan date. Selecting a day exposes the stored workout source and exact planned-versus-completed workout, exercise, and set progress. The Diary also shows this progress for its selected day. The chronology beneath the calendar retains the detailed active-plan view. Exercise, total-set, and warm-up-set counts come from stored workout definitions and logged sessions; the interface and coach never infer them from workout names or descriptions.
